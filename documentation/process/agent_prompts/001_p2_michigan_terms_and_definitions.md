# 001 · Part 2 · Michigan sports betting data: terms of use, provenance and definitions

Launched 2026-11-23 by the lead agent. Subagent type: general-purpose (web research, read only). Adapts course Prompts 2.1 and 2.4 (Verify: quote the sentence relied on, with its address).

## Context
A business analytics student is testing whether a public data source can support a course project. The source is the Michigan Gaming Control Board (MGCB) monthly "Internet Sports Betting" revenue spreadsheets, one .xlsx per year for 2021 to 2026, linked from https://www.michigan.gov/mgcb/detroit-casinos/resources/revenues-and-wagering-tax-information . Each file reports, per licensed operator per month: total handle, gross sports betting receipts, adjusted gross sports betting receipts, and state tax or payment. There is no separate free-play column; the project would derive "deductions" as gross receipts minus adjusted gross receipts. The course scores a source on license (terms allow academic use and say whether reposting is allowed), documentation (the publisher defines the variables), and provenance (who collected the data and how).

## Ask
Find and report, from primary pages you actually open:
1. Terms of use / copyright / reuse policy that apply to files on michigan.gov and specifically to MGCB data, if any. Is academic use allowed? Is reposting the files in a public GitHub repository allowed, forbidden, or not stated?
2. The legal definitions of "sports betting gross receipts" (or "gross sports betting receipts") and "adjusted gross sports betting receipts" in Michigan's Lawful Sports Betting Act (2019 PA 149, MCL 432.401 and following), including exactly what may be deducted (free play, promotional credits, federal excise tax, anything else), and any limits or timing rules on those deductions. Check the MGCB administrative rules for internet sports betting as well if the statute points to them.
3. How the data are collected: who reports to the MGCB, how often, and under which provision. Also the tax or payment rates that apply to commercial (Detroit casino) operators and to tribal operators, and any city tax.
4. Whether MGCB publishes any data dictionary, notes page, glossary or revenue-report explainer that defines the four columns (total handle, gross sports betting receipts, adjusted gross sports betting receipts, state tax/payment).
5. Any MGCB press release or statement explaining unusually large deductions in a single month (for example BetMGM, June 2024), since that affects how the deductions column should be read.
6. The citation form the publisher asks for, if any.

## Constraints
- Report only what is on pages you opened in this search. For every claim give the page address and quote the exact sentence you relied on (short quotes). If a page does not say, write "not stated". Do not estimate and do not fill gaps from general knowledge.
- Prefer primary sources: michigan.gov, legislature.mi.gov, the Michigan Administrative Code. A news article may be cited only for item 5 and must be labeled as secondary.
- Read only. Do not create accounts, sign in, accept terms, submit forms or download large files. Do not write or edit any files on this computer; return your findings in your final message.
- If a site blocks you (for example an HTTP 403), say so and name the page; do not substitute a guess.

## Verify
End with a table: item (1 to 6) · finding in one line · page address · the quoted sentence · "verified on page" or "not stated" or "blocked". Then list anything you could not verify and where a person could look to settle it.
